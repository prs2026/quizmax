# Example set: use one term | definition per line.
Cell | The smallest structural and functional unit of a living organism
Nucleus | The organelle that contains most of a cell's genetic material
Mitochondrion | An organelle that produces ATP through cellular respiration
Ribosome | The cellular structure that builds proteins
Chloroplast | The organelle where photosynthesis occurs in plants and algae
Cell membrane | The selective barrier that surrounds a cell
Osmosis | The movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane
Diffusion | The net movement of particles from higher to lower concentration
